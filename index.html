About me

A little about me.

The work I enjoy most sits right where code meets hardware: getting a microcontroller's peripherals to cooperate, making a design pass simulation before it ever touches a board, and chasing the bug that only shows up on real hardware.

On UGA IEEE's Micromouse team, I work on the firmware and maze-solving algorithms for an autonomous robot built around an STM32. Outside of class I'm a Senior Student Assistant at UGA Libraries and a host at Carrabba's. I'm also a Zell Miller Scholar and an Eagle Scout.
