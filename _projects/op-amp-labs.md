## Overview

Electronics I is a hands-on lab course in analog circuit design. For each lab I build op-amp circuits on the bench, measure how they actually behave, and compare the measurements to what hand analysis predicts.

## What I do

- **Build and measure.** I build op-amp circuits on the bench and measure their behavior with an oscilloscope.
- **Compare to theory.** I check each measurement against my hand analysis.
- **Write it up.** I document procedures, measured data, and analysis in technical lab reports written in LaTeX.

## Why it matters for embedded work

Firmware talks to the real world through analog front ends: sensor conditioning, filtering, and signal levels. Knowing how those circuits behave, and how to measure them, makes it much easier to tell a software bug from a hardware one.
